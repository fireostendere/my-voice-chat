LiveKit Companion is ready to use as a regular Windows application.

Open LiveKit Companion from the desktop or Start menu. Its control panel starts the
background service automatically, lets you capture the global push-to-talk key, sends
magnet links or .torrent files to an active room, and controls playback there.

No batch files, command prompts, or manual configuration are required. The companion
keeps running in the background and places the LiveKit icon in the system tray.
Double-click that icon to reopen the control panel, or right-click it to open or exit.

You can also uninstall it from Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > LiveKit Companion.
